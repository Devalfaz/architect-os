# Idea Template

*Capture ideas in 5 minutes. No commitment. The kill-criteria field is the important one — it prevents zombie projects.*

---

```markdown
# Idea: [TITLE]

<!-- date: YYYY-MM-DD -->

## The problem

[Who has this problem? What evidence do you have?]

## The user

[Who would use this? Be specific — "people who..." not "everyone"]

## What success looks like

[Measurable outcome. "Users can export their data as CSV in under 30 seconds"]

## Kill criteria

[What would make you drop this? Specific, verifiable. "If fewer than 5 users request this by March."]

## Rough scope

[Is this a weekend prototype, a month-long feature, or a platform?]

## Notes

[Anything else — related ideas, concerns, prior art]
```

---

*Save as `docs/product/ideas/<slug>.md`. Reviewed at weekly triage. Promoted ideas move to S1 (BRD). Killed ideas get `[KILLED]` prefix with reason — never delete.*
